## **1. Executive summary** 

Hobsons Bay Chess Club will be the host for Oceania youth championship 2027 in Melbourne. 

The Oceania Youth Zonal Championship represents the premier youth chess competition in the Oceania region. It brings together the strongest junior players from national federations including: 

1. Australia 

2. New Zealand 

3. Fiji 

4. Papua New Guinea 

5. Solomon Islands 

6. Palau 

7. Guam 

8. Nauru 

9. New Caledonia 

10. Tonga 

11. Vanuatu 

12. Kiribati 

13. Marshall Islands 

A five-day international youth chess championship will feature a classical tournament alongside rapid and blitz competitions. 

The event will follow FIDE regulations and provide a professional environment 

supporting competitive excellence, youth development, and international collaboration. 

Key strengths of this event include: 

- Proven experience delivering large-scale chess tournaments 

- A modern venue capable of hosting 400+ players 

- An experienced organising team including FIDE International Arbiters 

- Digital tournament operations and live broadcast capabilities 

- Strong community and institutional support within Melbourne

## **12. Federation Support Programme** 

To encourage broad participation from across the Oceania region, Hobsons Bay Chess Club (HBCC) is committed to supporting participating national federations, particularly developing Pacific Island federations.

The programme is designed to reduce participation barriers, encourage international 

representation, and strengthen chess development throughout Oceania. 

Support initiatives are subject to available funding, sponsorship and organiser capacity. 

### **12.1 Official Federation Delegation** 

Participating federations may nominate an official delegation comprising: 

- Up to **one (1) Official Player in each age category** 

- **One (1) Head of Delegation** 

Official players will participate as recognised representatives of their respective national federations. 

The Organising Committee reserves the right to determine the final number of supported officials based on available resources. 

### **12.2 Accommodation Support** 

HBCC intends to provide complimentary accommodation for official federation delegations comprising: 

- One (1) Official Player per age category 

- One (1) Head of Delegation per federation 

Accommodation will generally be provided on a twin-share basis for the official duration of the championship. 

This initiative aims to significantly reduce travel costs for participating federations and encourage wider regional participation.

### **12.3 Pacific Island Federation Participation Assistance Scheme** 

To encourage participation from developing Oceania federations and assist with 

regional travel costs, HBCC will establish a **Pacific Island Federation Participation** 

##### **Assistance Scheme** . 

Under this scheme: 

- The **first three (3) eligible federations** will each receive a **$1,000 travel assistance grant** . 

- A maximum of **three grants** will be awarded. 

#### **Eligibility** 

To qualify, a federation must: 

- Be a member federation of the Oceania Chess Confederation (OCC). 

- Be located outside Australia and New Zealand. 

- Register a delegation of at least **five (5) players** . 

- Submit the names of all participating players to the Organising Committee by **1 March 2027** . 

- Be among the first three federations meeting all eligibility requirements. 

#### **Payment Conditions** 

The travel assistance grant will: 

- Be paid only after the federation has completed in-person registration at the championship venue. 

- Be paid to an authorised federation representative or by another method approved by the Organising Committee. 

- Be forfeited if the federation withdraws, fails to attend, or no longer satisfies the eligibility requirements.

- General travel and logistics advice. 

- Accommodation coordination assistance. 

- Information on local transport options. 

- Airport transfer and hotel-to-venue shuttle services for official delegations (subject to availability and funding). 

- General guidance on applying for available FIDE and Oceania development grants. 

Visa invitation letters will only be provided to both official and unofficial players and accompanying persons (e.g. parents) once the entry and registration fee has been paid. If you are unable to obtain a visa, your entry/registration fee will be refunded.

### **12.5 Special Prize for Pacific Island Federation Players** 

To encourage greater participation and recognise talented young players from across the Pacific, the Oceania Youth Chess Championship 2026 will offer Special Prizes for Pacific Island Federation Players. 

These prizes are open to eligible players representing Oceania chess federations other than Australia and New Zealand. 

The prizes will recognise the best-performing eligible Pacific Island Federation players participating in the Championship and are separate from the main Championship titles and other age-group prizes. 

Eligibility and final prize allocation will be determined by the tournament organisers.
