Official Bulletin

Official Tournament Bulletin

  • 01Official Dates Confirmed: Oceania Youth Zonal 2027 will be held 27 September – 4 October 2027 at Bacchus Marsh Grammar – Woodlea Campus in Melbourne, Australia.
  • 023-in-1 Championship Package: Registration includes entry into all three championships (Classical 9 Rounds, Rapid 7 Rounds, and Blitz 9 Rounds) with NO extra event fees.
  • 03Pacific Island Assistance Scheme: The first 3 eligible federations (outside AUS & NZ) registering 5+ players by 1 March 2027 will receive a $1,000 AUD travel grant.
  • 04Official Federation Delegations receive complimentary twin-share accommodation for 1 Official Player per category plus 1 Head of Delegation.

Quick Reference

Everything Families & Players Need

01

Host Venue & City

Bacchus Marsh Grammar – Woodlea Campus (111 Frontier Ave, Aintree VIC 3336), Melbourne, Australia. Hosted by Hobsons Bay Chess Club Inc. (Est. 1974).

02

Official Dates

27 September – 4 October 2027. Classical rounds run Sep 28 – Oct 3. Rapid & Blitz run Oct 3.

03

Championship Format

FIDE Rated Classical (9 Rounds, 90m+30s), Rapid (7 Rounds, 10m+2s), and Blitz (9 Rounds, 3m+2s).

04

Eligible Federations

Australia, New Zealand, Fiji, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Palau, Guam, Nauru, New Caledonia, Tonga, Vanuatu, Kiribati, and Marshall Islands.

Championship Honors

Titles, Medals & Norm Opportunities

FIDE Direct Titles

Direct GM, WGM, IM, WIM, FM, WFM, CM, and WCM titles and norms awarded per FIDE Regulations B/01.

Trophies & Medals

Official Gold, Silver, and Bronze trophies for all 14 championships, plus Pacific Island Special Prizes.

Regional Glory

Compete for your national federation on Oceania's biggest junior stage.

First 300 Lucky Draw

First 300 registered players enter a draw for 5x DGT Chess Clocks and 1x ChessNut Go electronic board.
