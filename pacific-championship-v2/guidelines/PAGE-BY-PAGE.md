# Page-by-page composition

| Page | New visual structure |
|---|---|
| Home `/` | Poster-like typographic event lead and custom art panel; full-width metrics; entry band moved earlier; slim clock; numbered editorial bulletin; large age tabs; practical guide; honours/experience spread; snapshot; retained original banner; support split. |
| Schedule `/schedule` | Oversized title with summary in the opposite column; a left day rail and ruled date/title/time rows; calendar as a small utility action. Day rail becomes a horizontal control area on phone. |
| Regulations `/regulations` | Document cover, compact facts, a persistent contents margin and a readable rulebook column. No card around every rule. |
| Registration `/registration` | Entry worksheet and large typographic payable total on the left; interest form in a separated right column; grants and lucky-draw information below. |
| Players `/players` | Left filter rail, horizontal age index and structured player records, using the existing public players.json snapshot. Names and data are not invented. |
| Visit `/location` | Venue/address spread opposite a large map; practical travel notes; separate accommodation/dining columns. |
| News `/news` | Featured editorial story with a large signature motif; ruled date/headline/excerpt rows rather than a card grid. |
| Article `/news/2026-08-19-the-road-to-oceania-2027-begins` | Large editorial headline, byline/date and a calm long-form column. Original article body preserved. |
| Article `/news/2026-07-03-planning-update` | Same article system with the original shorter body. |
| Article `/news/2026-07-02-welcome-update` | Same article system with the original launch copy. |
| Organisers `/organizer` | Introductory statement followed by role/name lists; restrained hierarchy and long rules. |
| Sponsors `/sponsors` | Typography-led partner wall, equal spacing and minimal decorative event marks. No fabricated official sponsor logos. |
| Broadcasts `/dgt-boards` | Chessboard becomes the main object; notation and board choices form a side rail; external feed index below. Original teaser data is kept as teaser content. |
| Contact `/contact-us` | Underlined form fields on the left and committee details on a distinct margin at right; no floating form cards. |

The route names and original destinations are retained. The public-facing wording, fees and dates were not reconciled or rewritten, including existing inconsistencies. The original banner image and PDFs remain unchanged.
