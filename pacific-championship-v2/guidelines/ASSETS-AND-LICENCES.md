# Assets and licences

Original V2 vector designs: mark.svg, mark-mono.svg, pacific-court.svg and ocean-lines.svg. These are editable SVG artwork created for this project. Event marks are decorative brand concepts, not official federation logos or endorsements.

Barlow Condensed Bold and Manrope are self-hosted from the Google Fonts repository, under the SIL Open Font Licence. The corresponding OFL texts are included next to their font files. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope .

Country silhouettes are simplified from Natural Earth's country data via https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries . Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Shapes are decorative silhouettes, normalised individually; they are not scale/position/border-authority maps.

Flag SVGs are retained from https://flagcdn.com/ for AU, NZ, FJ, PG, SB, PW, GU, NR, NC, TO, VU, KI and MH. Service information: https://flagpedia.net/download/api .

Existing banner/hero images and tournament PDFs are copied unchanged from the user's website. The original content and its third-party rights remain with their respective owners. Existing source HTML is retained privately for content comparison, not as a grant of third-party rights.

players.json was downloaded unchanged from the existing public tournament endpoint. It is a snapshot of that source; no extra player fields are shown beyond the existing directory's public presentation.
