Getting to Melbourne

Airport Connections & Local Transit

✈️ International Air Travel

Melbourne Airport (Tullamarine - MEL) is ~25-30 minutes drive via the Western Ring Road (M80) and Western Highway (M8). Avalon Airport (AVV) is ~35-40 minutes drive via M1/M80.

🚆 Trains, Trams & SkyBus

Take the V/Line Ballarat Line train from Southern Cross Station in Melbourne CBD to Rockbank Station. Bus or short taxi/rideshare connects Rockbank Station to Woodlea Campus on Frontier Avenue.

Recommended Transit Routes

V/Line Train (Southern Cross -> Rockbank Station)

Ballarat Line direct service from Melbourne CBD to Rockbank Station (~25 mins).

Driving via M8 Western Highway (Rockbank Exit)

Exit M8 at Leakes Road in Rockbank and follow signs onto Frontier Avenue in Woodlea.
