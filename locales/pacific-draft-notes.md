# Pacific language AI drafts for federation review

These dictionaries are AI-generated working drafts, not human-reviewed or federation-approved translations. A translated phrase is not evidence of native accuracy. The website must keep the visible AI-draft notice when any of these locales is selected.

Each JSON file is a flat map from rendered English source text to a draft translation. Keys use trimmed, collapsed whitespace. Missing strings deliberately fall back to English. Counts below describe dictionary entries, not the percentage of the site translated or language quality. Do not infer coverage from the similar counts: each phrase needs review in its actual interface context.

| File | Language | Entries | Review priorities and limitations |

Coverage prioritizes the actual site shell and navigation, the home actions and selected short headings, search, contact form labels, schedule filters, player filters, registration actions, and broadcast/replay controls. Proper names, chess notation, URLs, dates, numbers, federation identifiers, and tournament identifiers are retained where present.

Long legal or regulatory passages, financial eligibility conditions, visa advice, third-party feeds, news articles, and most travel prose are intentionally absent. Short fee-related labels do not translate the surrounding financial conditions. Dynamic text may remain English when the completed rendered string has no exact key; unsupported text must not be presented as translated or as an error.

Federation reviewers should review wording in context before adding approval status, identify preferred chess and computing terminology, check that labels remain understandable on small screens, and supply replacements or additional phrases as needed. There is no native-speaker or federation sign-off in this draft.
